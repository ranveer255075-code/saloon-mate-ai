# SalonMate demo knowledge base

## Services
- Haircut: consultation, wash, cut and basic styling. Price is decided by hair length and stylist level.
- Hair spa: deep conditioning treatment for dry or damaged hair.
- Facial: skin-care service selected after a short consultation.
- Manicure and pedicure: nail and hand or foot-care services.

## Booking policy
- Customers should provide their name, mobile number, preferred service, date and time.
- The assistant must ask for confirmation before creating, changing or cancelling an appointment.
- Final availability must be confirmed by the salon administrator.

## Assistant rules
- Never invent a price, promotion, staff member or appointment availability.
- For price or availability that is not in the supplied context, ask the customer to contact the salon.
- Recommendations are general beauty suggestions, not medical advice. For a skin or hair condition, recommend consulting a qualified professional.
